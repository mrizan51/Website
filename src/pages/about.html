Our Story

Built in the heart of Kerala's marine export belt.

Premier Exports International was established in {{site.founded}} at Aroor — the narrow strip of land between Kochi's harbour and the Vembanad backwaters that grew into India's most concentrated seafood processing cluster. We began as packers of shrimp and cephalopods for discerning buyers in Japan, and grew the way lasting export houses do: shipment by shipment, relationship by relationship.

Today we process and export more than thirty-five species — shrimp, lobster, crab, clams, squid, cuttlefish, octopus and premium fish — from an EU-approved facility operating under HACCP. Our three house brands, Prime, Premier and Primus, are carried by importers across Japan, Europe and the USA, with growing business in the Gulf and China.

Through every season and every market cycle, the principle has stayed the same: buy the best raw material on the day, handle it with care, grade it honestly, and ship it on time.
