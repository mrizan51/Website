Our Story

From Chandiroor to tables across the world.

Premier Exports International was established in {{site.founded}} by Mr. K. M. Abdulla and Mr. A. Musthafa in Chandiroor, Kerala — on the narrow strip of coast between Kochi's harbour and the Vembanad backwaters that became India's most concentrated seafood processing cluster. They began as packers of shrimp and cephalopods for discerning buyers in Japan, and grew the way lasting export houses do: shipment by shipment, relationship by relationship.

Today the company is managed by the next generation — Mr. Salim K. A., Mr. Mohammed Rizan Salim and Mr. Nissam Musthafa — who continue the founders' legacy while investing in modern processing, certification and traceability. Our three house brands, Prime, Premier and Primus, are carried by importers across Japan, Europe and the Middle East.

Through every season and every market cycle, the principle has stayed the same: buy the best raw material on the day, handle it with care, grade it honestly, and ship it on time.
