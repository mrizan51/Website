Our Process

From landing to loading, under control.

Raw material selection

Buyers at harbours and farm gates along the Kerala coast select to our specification — species, size, freshness — on the day of landing.

Iced transport

Insulated, iced transport brings raw material to Aroor within hours, with temperature checked at receiving.

Processing & grading

Cleaning, peeling, filleting and hand-grading in hygienic processing halls under HACCP monitoring.

Freezing

Blast, plate and IQF freezing to the core temperature your specification demands — fast enough to preserve texture and bloom.

Cold storage

Finished cartons held at −18 °C or below, lot-coded and positioned for orderly container stuffing.

Inspection & dispatch

Pre-shipment inspection, export documentation and sealed reefer dispatch through Cochin Port.

Traceability

Every lot traces back to landing centre or farm, harvest date and processing batch — supporting catch certificates and recalls drills alike.

Testing

Microbiological and antibiotic-residue testing through approved laboratories, with certificates of analysis accompanying shipments on request.
